How it works

  1. 01

    Snap

    Point the camera at a receipt, or import a photo from your gallery. Crumpled and faded both work.

  2. 02

    Confirm

    Parse extracts the merchant, date, total and line items. Review and edit anything before saving — it's your bookkeeping, not the model's.

  3. 03

    Forget about it

    Receipts sort themselves into categories, stay searchable, and export to spreadsheet or PDF when tax time comes around.

Built for people who lose receipts

Freelancers, small businesses, and anyone who has ever emptied a jacket pocket in March and found a faded blur where a client dinner used to be.

  • Camera-first. The app is the camera. Nothing stands between you and the receipt.
  • Works offline. Scan on the plane; it processes when you land.
  • Actually searchable. By merchant, date or category — not an album of photos you'll never open.
  • Exports properly. Spreadsheet or PDF, in a shape your accountant won't complain about.

About account emails

Parse sends only transactional email that you trigger: sign-in codes, account and security notices, and exports you ask for. We don't send marketing email, and we don't sell or share email addresses. Questions or opt-out requests go to support@parseai.app.
