Support

Questions, bug reports, data requests or email opt-outs: write to support@parseai.app. We aim to reply within two business days.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Menu → Settings and choose Delete account. Or email us and we'll do it for you.

How do I export my receipts?

Open the Export screen in the app and choose spreadsheet or PDF.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.
